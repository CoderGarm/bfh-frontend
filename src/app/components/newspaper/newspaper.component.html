The primary objective of Operation War Harvest '24 was the eradication of the notorious pirate organization known as the Kersey Association. The remaining targets have withdrawn from public view, presenting a challenge for future actions.

The operation commenced with a coordinated assault on multiple fronts, utilizing a combination of stealth, strategic intelligence, and overwhelming force. INTERCEPT Forces engaged in a relentless pursuit, pushing the Kersey Association into retreat.

- Captured: 25
- Remaining: Withdrawn from public eye

- Combat vessels: 6.1 million tons


B. Casualties

1. Personnel:
- INTERCEPT Forces: Countless brave soldiers

2. Civilian Impact:
- Minimal collateral damage, with efforts made to safeguard civilian populations

IV. Operation War Harvest '24 Assessment

A. Successes

1. Pirate Base Capture:
The capture of 25 pirate bases severely hinders the operational capacity of the Kersey Association.

2. Enemy Ship Losses:
The destruction of 6.1 million tons of pirate warships significantly weakens their military capabilities.

B. Challenges

1. Remaining Targets:
The withdrawal of the remaining pirate targets necessitates ongoing efforts to neutralize hidden threats.

2. Friendly Losses:
The loss of 3.5 million tons of INTERCEPT Forces' assets is a setback that requires strategic reassessment.

A. Intelligence Operations

1. Unveiling Hidden Targets:
Ongoing intelligence efforts are crucial for uncovering the whereabouts of the remaining pirate bases.

2. Strategic Realignment:
Evaluate and adjust our strategies to address the evolving tactics of the Kersey Association.

Undertake efforts to recover and repair damaged assets.

2. International Cooperation:
Strengthen collaboration among participating nations to bolster future joint operations.

VI. Conclusion

Operation War Harvest '24, while successful in dismantling a significant portion of the Kersey Association's infrastructure, presents ongoing challenges. The INTERCEPT Forces remain vigilant, committed to ensuring the security and stability of the Sol System against piracy threats.
