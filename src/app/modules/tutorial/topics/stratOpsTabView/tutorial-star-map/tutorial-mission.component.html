Overview

Mission are a way to let actions be done from your navy or other related parts of the executing forces.
You can hunt pirates automatically in the orbits of your planets and protect the full system by reducing the pirates influence.

Pirate Hunt

Your planet's gouverneurs are, of course, emotionally related to their planets and systems but from time to time they are right: Protect them!

Every inhabited planet will be visited by pirates and let them know, that there is something to steal.
This will affect the security of your convoys, too.

Convoy protection

Trade routes have been subject to attacks at all times.
Of course, this will not stop in space.

You are invited to secure your trade routes by your navy. The space is scary enough to state that nearly every convoy is attacked.

Ensure that your systems are safe places and not juicy prey.
