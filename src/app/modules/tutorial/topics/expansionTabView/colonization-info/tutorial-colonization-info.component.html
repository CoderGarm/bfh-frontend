Overview

In order to gain influence in the ranks of emperors and kings it is necessary to project power.
As long as power comes from warships, two planets can maintain more shipyard capacity than one.

Colonizations

A colonization is simple, you've done one not long ago. Take a ship, pack some people and constructions and boldy go, where no one has gone before.

Planned colonizations

A colonization is an expensive project.
It takes some time until your people are moved to your home world. If everyone is present, the ship will start.
