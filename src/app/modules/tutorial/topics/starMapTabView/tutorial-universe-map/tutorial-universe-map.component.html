The galaxy is segmented by a polar coordinate system based at Sol, the home of the mankind.

Navigation at the map

Use the mouse to drag the map and the mouse wheel to zoom to or from the pointer.
Hover over systems to see their name, click them to enter the System Map.

The search field at the bottom allows you to search systems by name and zoom at them.

Fleets on the map

The fleet icons on the map are displaying their current position without time dilation. They show also the state, if the fleet is moving or inoperational.
But keep in mind that you need an eye if you want to have intel.

Moving fleets

Move your fleets by marking them and the designated target.
The movement option in the notch will be accessible to start the movement.

As known, it is pretty hard to find a fleet in hyperspace and therefore it is impossible to cancel a given movement order.
Movements with sub-light speed can be cancelled at any time.
