Spot market

While your small, independent and insignificant empire is not part of a bigger trade alliance it is pretty useful to have access to the resource market.

Offer market

You have too much resources? Sell it! In the end you can buy them back but money rules the world.

Trading times and distances

The trade market is part of a incredibly big trading network where freighters are flying constantly from and to every point in the known galaxy.

If you offer a piece of your cake or buy another one's, you are getting a bit from that warehouse and this and that freighter from half a dozens convoys.
The private trading business is much bigger than you can imagine and there are trading partners you never heard from, crossing your territory.
All these trades are part of a galaxy-wide network and can be rearranged from professional brokers to fit your needs.
