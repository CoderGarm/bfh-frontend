Select an amount of ships to build.

Fleet integration

While building the ships will be assigned to a 'virtual fleet'. If there is a real fleet in orbit while finishing all the ships of a job at once, they will be assigned to the biggest fleet.

Operationals

Please keep in mind that ships will be constructed, no matter if there are enough crew to man it. The finished ships without a crew will stay inoperational until the crew has arrived or is educated.

Orbiting fleets

Fleets are ammoed up when they are in orbit for a longer period of time.

They can also be repaired, but this has to be done manually and will interrupt the current shipyard's job.
Upgrading the fleets ships to the latest flight is done the same way.

Please keep in mind that a fleet will be deactivated through the yard's work. Afterwards it will be reactivated if the personell is present.

Bought ship classes

Your started the colonization of your man planet with a bought flotilla long ago. These bought ships can't be build until you have researched all compartments.
