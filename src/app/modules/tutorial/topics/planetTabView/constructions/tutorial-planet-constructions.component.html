Construct Buildings

All the buildings describes a planet-wide network of facilities in the same business area. For instance, the next level of a university means not necessarily a new campus in the capital city but probably a small laboratory in the swamp outside.

Operationals

Please keep in mind that buildings will be constructed, no matter if there are enough workers to man it. The finished level without a crew will stay inoperational until the workers has arrived or is educated.

Inoperational levels will be displayed at the infrastructure dashboard. The inoperational levels don't produce anything until they are activated.
