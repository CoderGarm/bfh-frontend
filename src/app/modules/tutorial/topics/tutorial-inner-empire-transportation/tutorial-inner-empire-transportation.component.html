Demand & Deliveries

While you can move stuff with transport ships everywhere, inside your empire is a special ability present.
Some sort of companies are specialized to deliveries - you can address your wishes to them and they will execute the movements every tick.

Chosen amounts

You must not match the precise amounts between delivery possibilities and demands. The demand will be only fulfilled, if there are open delivery positions.
Never will be moved more than offered.

Ship transfers

The mothballed ships can be transferred to other planets. Just dran them to their designated destinations.

Fast services

At the moment there are a lot of missed balancing around the game and until the the final balancing is done, the delivery companies have magical carriages.

They can be everywhere tomorrow.

One time service

The defined resource delivery plans will be just executed once. They will be zero'ed then.
If it is not possible to execute a movement plan, they will be zero'ed anyways.
